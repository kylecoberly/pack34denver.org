Set Up Your Scout’s Trail’s End Account

Setting up your online store allows out-of-town family, friends, and neighbors to buy popcorn online or donate credit directly to our pack. It takes less than 5 minutes!

  1. Download the App or Go Online
    Download the Trail's End app on your phone (iOS / Android) or register on the web.
  2. Connect to Pack 34
    During registration, select Register as a Scout and pick our unit code: AUUA8
  3. Start Sharing & Selling
    • Direct Share: Share your Scout's personal sales link or QR code via email or social media.
    • Storefront Shifts: Sign up for shifts at local storefronts organized by the Pack in the app.
    • Wagon Sales: Take digital credit card orders door-to-door using the app checkout.
